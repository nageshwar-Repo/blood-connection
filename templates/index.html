For donors

Donation safety & preparation

General guidance only. Local blood-service screening determines eligibility and safe donation intervals.

Ask the assistant

Eligibility screening

Age, weight, hemoglobin, health, medicines, recent travel, and other factors can affect eligibility. Rules vary by location; trained staff make the final decision.

Before donating

Have a normal meal, drink water, get adequate rest, and bring the identification requested by the camp. Tell staff about medicines or recent illness.

After donating

Rest, take fluids and a snack, and avoid strenuous activity for the rest of the day. Tell staff promptly if you feel faint or unwell.
